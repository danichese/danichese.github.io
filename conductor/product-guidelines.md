# Product Guidelines

## Tone & Voice
-   **Professional & Expert:** The content must convey technical authority and industry competence, positioning the developer as a senior-level problem solver.
-   **Forward-Thinking:** While professional, the voice should confidently articulate the benefits of AI-assisted development without overhyping.

## Visual Identity
-   **Aesthetic:** "High-Tech & Futuristic" blended with "Human-Centric" elements.
    -   *Tech:* Use a dark mode base with subtle glowing accents or data-inspired motifs to signal "AI."
    -   *Human:* Balance the tech with warm accent colors or typography that feels approachable, ensuring the site doesn't feel cold or robotic.
-   **Responsiveness:** Retain and refine the distinct mobile vs. desktop experiences. The design must be adaptive, not just fluid.
-   **Portfolio as Proof:** The portfolio site *itself* is the primary demonstration of frontend design skills. It should be polished, fast, and visually impressive.

## Project Presentation
-   **Focus:** Prioritize the new AI/Full Stack projects (PSP-Lab, Security Service Decomposition).
-   **Differentiation:** Do not clutter the interface with old projects. Let the new work stand as the "Hero" content.
-   **Contextual Depth:** Use the rich context from the existing CV to flesh out project descriptions and the "About" section, ensuring the narrative of "Knowledge Management -> Full Stack Engineer" is clear.

## CV & Artifacts
-   **Workflow:**
    -   *Input:* The existing PDF CV serves as the raw context/source material.
    -   *Output:* A polished Markdown CV is the final deliverable artifact.
-   **Consistency:** The website and the Markdown CV should tell a unified story, but the CV will remain the formal, detailed record for applications.
